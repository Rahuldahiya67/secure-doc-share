Secure Doc Share

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

What this site does

Secure Doc Share lets a document owner share PDF files with specific email addresses. Only people whose verified email is on a document's allowed list can view it.

Information we collect

  • Your email address and name, from Google sign-in or the email sign-in link. We use it only to check whether you are allowed to view a document.
  • Viewing activity: which document you opened, when, how long you spent on each page, and your phone model with an app install ID. The document owner can see this activity.

We do not collect passwords, payment details, contacts or any other Google account data.

How it is used

  • To sign you in and decide which documents you can open.
  • To show your email as a watermark on documents you view.
  • To let the document owner see who viewed their documents.

We never sell or share your information with advertisers or other third parties.

Where data is stored

Sign-in, documents and view records are stored with Supabase. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Deleting your data
