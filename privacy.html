Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

What this site does

Pro AI Trainers lets a document owner share PDF files with specific email addresses. Only people whose verified email is on a document's allowed list can view it.

Information we collect

  • Your email address and name, from Google sign-in or the email sign-in link. We use it only to check whether you are allowed to view a document.
  • Payment submissions: if you submit a payment, your name, Outlier ID, who referred you, your payout date, the amounts you enter, how you paid and the UPI reference (UTR) number, and the screenshots you upload. We also keep a fingerprint of each screenshot to spot duplicates. This app never sees your bank, card or UPI PIN details; payments happen inside your own UPI app. Only you and the site owner can see them.
  • Viewing activity: which document you opened, when, how long you spent on each page, and your phone model with an app install ID. The document owner can see this activity.
  • Your profile: the name, phone number, Outlier ID and referrer you save under My account, used to fill in your payment form. Only you and the site owner can see it.
  • Phone ID: if the owner turns on the one-phone lock, the ID of the phone you read on, so a different phone needs the owner's approval.
  • Quizzes, feedback and requests: your quiz answers and scores, messages you send with Send feedback, and access requests. Only you and the site owner can see them.

We do not collect passwords, payment details, contacts or any other Google account data.

How it is used

  • To sign you in and decide which documents you can open.
  • To show your email as a watermark on documents you view.
  • To let the document owner see who viewed their documents.

We never sell or share your information with advertisers or other third parties.

Where data is stored

Sign-in, documents and view records are stored with Supabase. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages.
